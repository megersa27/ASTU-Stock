                         Introduction
1.1 Purpose of the Document
This document presents the product planning for the ASTU Stock Management System. It serves as the foundation of the project by explaining the purpose of the system, the business problem it addresses, and the overall direction of development.
The handbook is intended to guide the project team throughout the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC), from planning to deployment and maintenance.
1.2 Project Overview
The ASTU Stock Management System is a web-based application designed to help Adama Science and Technology University (ASTU) manage its inventory efficiently.
The system will allow authorized users to record, monitor, and manage stock items through a centralized digital platform. It will replace manual inventory management with a secure and user-friendly solution.
The project will be developed using modern software engineering practices, including the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) and Agile methodology.
1.3 Business Background
Inventory management is an essential activity in every organization. Universities purchase and distribute many types of items such as laboratory equipment, office supplies, furniture, computers, and other assets.
When inventory is managed manually using paper records or spreadsheets, several problems may occur, including inaccurate records, delays in reporting, and difficulty tracking stock movement.
A digital stock management system can improve efficiency, reduce errors, and provide accurate information for decision-making.
1.4 Existing Problem
The current inventory management process may experience the following challenges:
    • Manual recording of stock information. 
    • Difficulty tracking available inventory. 
    • Time-consuming searching for stock records. 
    • Human errors during data entry. 
    • Delayed report generation. 
    • Limited transparency in stock movement. 
    • Difficulty monitoring issued and received items. 
These challenges reduce operational efficiency and increase the risk of inventory inaccuracies.

1.5 Proposed Solution
The proposed solution is to develop a web-based Stock Management System that enables users to manage inventory digitally.
The system will provide features such as:
    • User authentication and authorization. 
    • Inventory management. 
    • Stock receiving. 
    • Stock issuing. 
    • Category management. 
    • Supplier management. 
    • Search functionality. 
    • Dashboard with summary statistics. 
    • Report generation. 
    • User management. 
The system will provide real-time access to inventory information and simplify daily operations.

1.6 Expected Benefits
The proposed system is expected to provide the following benefits:
For ASTU
    • Improve inventory accuracy. 
    • Reduce paperwork. 
    • Increase operational efficiency. 
    • Improve transparency. 
    • Generate reports quickly. 
    • Support better decision-making. 
For Users
    • Easy access to inventory information. 
    • Faster stock searching. 
    • Simplified inventory management. 
    • Reduced manual work. 
For Developers
    • Gain practical experience in modern software development. 
    • Apply Agile methodology. 
    • Practice Git and GitHub collaboration. 
    • Learn Docker-based deployment. 
    • Experience the complete Software Development Life Cycle. 

1.7 Project Objectives
The main objectives of the project are:
    • Develop a secure web-based stock management system. 
    • Digitize inventory management processes. 
    • Improve stock tracking and reporting. 
    • Reduce manual record keeping. 
    • Build the project using modern technologies.
