📚 Stock Management Manual (Summary)
Purpose of the Manual
This manual explains how government organizations in Ethiopia should manage stock (inventory).
Your software's job is to digitize these manual processes.
Think like this:
Manual (Paper Forms)
        ↓
Your Software
        ↓
Digital System

Chapter 1 — Introduction
What it teaches
This chapter answers:
What is stock management?
It defines terms like:
    • Stock 
    • Inventory 
    • Store 
    • Warehouse 
    • Property 
    • Supplier 
    • Receiving 
    • Issuing 
It also explains why organizations keep stock.
Example:
ASTU stores:
    • Computers 
    • Chairs 
    • Projectors 
    • Laboratory equipment 
    • Printer paper 
Without stock management:
❌ Items disappear
❌ Nobody knows quantities
❌ Money is wasted

Software Features From Chapter 1
Your system should include:
    • Dashboard 
    • Total stock 
    • Categories 
    • Current quantity 
    • Store information

Chapter 3 — Receiving & Inspection
This chapter explains what happens when ASTU buys new items.
Example:
ASTU buys
20 Computers.
Before storing them:
    • Count them 
    • Inspect them 
    • Confirm quality 
    • Record supplier 
    • Record purchase date 
Then save them into inventory.
